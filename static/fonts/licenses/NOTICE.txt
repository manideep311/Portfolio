The font files in static/fonts/ are subsets of the originals published by
Fontsource (fontsource.org): glyphs outside Latin, Latin-1, general
punctuation, arrows and a few symbols were removed (Special Elite keeps
ASCII plus a handful of accents and symbols) and hinting was stripped
to reduce download size (pyftsubset, fontTools). No glyph designs were
changed. Fraunces, Instrument Sans and Kalam remain under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; Special Elite remains under the Apache License 2.0.
